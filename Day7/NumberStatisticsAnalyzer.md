# Exercise 1: Number Statistics Analyzer

## Objective

Continuously ask the user to enter numbers.

Entering **0** ends the program.

### Example

```text
Please enter a number (0 to finish):
10
20
30
15
8
0
```

### Expected Output

```text
You entered 5 numbers.

Sum: 83
Average: 16.6
Largest: 30
Smallest: 8
Even numbers: 3
Odd numbers: 2
```

## Requirements

Use variables such as:

```python
count
total
largest
smallest
even_count
odd_count
```

Do **not** use a List.

## Things to Think About

1. How should you initialize `largest` and `smallest`?
2. When should you calculate the average?
3. How can you determine whether a number is even or odd?

## Bonus Challenges

- Count how many numbers are greater than 100.
- Count how many negative numbers were entered.
- Calculate the average of numbers less than or equal to 100.
